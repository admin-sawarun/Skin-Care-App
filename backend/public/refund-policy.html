Refund Policy

Lock and Key Skin Care · Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Refund Policy explains when you are eligible for a refund of a consultation fee paid through the Lock and Key Skin Care app, operated by SAWARUN TECH PVT LTD ("we", "us", "our").

1. When You're Eligible for a Refund

  • Payment was deducted from your account, but your case was not successfully submitted due to a technical error on our end
  • No doctor is assigned to your case within 24 hours of a successful payment
  • You were charged more than once for the same case submission (duplicate payment)

2. When Refunds Are Not Provided

  • Once a doctor has reviewed your case and provided a diagnosis, prescription or any consultation response, the fee covers that professional service and is non-refundable
  • Change of mind after a doctor has already been assigned to your case
  • Dissatisfaction with a doctor's medical opinion or advice — this is a clinical matter, not a billing one; please raise a support ticket in-app so we can look into it separately

3. How to Request a Refund

Contact us at admin-sawarun@savarun.in, or raise a support ticket from within the App, within 7 days of the payment. Please include your registered phone number and the case ID (if available).

4. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method (UPI, card or netbanking account used at checkout) within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy, contact us at admin-sawarun@savarun.in.
